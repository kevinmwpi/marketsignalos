## What it means for D4

- **The minimum is not what blocks wallets.** For wallets with a positive mean,
  the lower bound needs a median of about 13 effective observations. That is close
  to `MIN_CLV_SAMPLE = 10`, so lowering the minimum would admit almost no one who
  also clears the bound. One wallet clears the bound below 10.
- **The mean is what blocks them.** The median wallet's 1 h CLV is −0.69¢, which is
  consistent with paying about half the spread on entry (D3). Only 8 of 25 wallets
  are positive. That is the hard bar the plan expected.
- **Collection still limits the sample.** 7,611 eligible fills await an entry-price
  chunk. Fills on open and exited bets are also not prioritised: `priority_chunks`
  covers the horizon diagnostic's resolved bets only. Most wallets were added within
  the last two days. The median sample of 3 will grow without any threshold change.
- **D4 stands: keep 10.** The plan's guess of about 70 needed observations assumed a
  5¢ standard deviation of bet CLV; the measured 2.7¢ makes significance reachable. What blocks wallets is
  a negative mean. Changing the threshold would not change that.

## Caveats

- One report, one day after the cohort changed (profit seeding on 2026-10-03, bots
  removed 2026-10-04 and 2026-10-05). The cohort is selected on recent winning
  (blueprint decision 6, seeding change). These are descriptions of the cohort,
  not evidence of skill.
- The standard deviation is the median over wallets with at least two effective
  observations.

## Recommendations

1. **Step 4: switch the pilot to forecast-v5.** The counts above show the switch
   changes no tailability today, and it replaces a gate that restates the outcome
   with one that does not. It has to land before the Stage 3 freeze. The dashboard
   keeps its labels: it reads the research snapshot, not the pilot.
2. **Fetch v5's fills first.** Extend the entry-price priority to every fill at
   least seven days before its scheduled end, open and exited bets included, so
   `not_fetched` drains.
3. Re-read this report weekly. Re-decide D4 only if `not_fetched` is near zero and
   wallets with positive means still sit below 10.
